About

Made by Tyler McIntyre to pick sessions faster at Money20/20 USA. It is not affiliated with Money20/20.

Privacy. Your picks stay in this browser. The app only talks to the internet to fetch the schedule and, when you tap a calendar button, to build your calendar feed (it sends the IDs of your saved sessions to m2020.pages.dev — nothing else).

Session times are shown in Pacific Time (Las Vegas).
